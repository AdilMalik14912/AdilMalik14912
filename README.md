## Executive Bio & Core Focus

I am a **Full-Stack Developer and Entrepreneur** based in New Delhi, India. I specialize in building scalable enterprise systems, logistics networks, clean energy infrastructure, and modern web applications.

- **Building ParcelUncle & Courier Uncle & Lekya Logistics**: Hyperlocal & Pan-India courier aggregation and logistics management platform (Django 5.2, Next.js 14, DRF, Redis, Celery, PostgreSQL).
- **Building Lekya Energy**: CleanTech and energy solutions infrastructure powering sustainable power management systems.
- **Building lekya.in**: Premium online eyewear e-commerce platform with AI-assisted sizing and lens customization.
- **Specializing in**: Multi-tenant architectures, real-time GPS tracking, automated rider dispatch, OTP delivery verification, B2B credit billing, and high-concurrency REST APIs.
- **Ask me about**: Python, Django, DRF, Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Systems Architecture, and Payment Gateways.
